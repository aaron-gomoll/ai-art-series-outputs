AI Art Series — Output Progression

14 curated runs · Finding the model ceiling, then breaking it

Overview

Goal
Generate a print-ready art series for a kid's room — images that read as a unified design system — from a simple design brief. Validation / proof-of-concept for a consumer "Create Your Own Series" experience.
AI Challenge
Image models are non-deterministic and hard to evaluate. A coherent series needs consistency across theme, color, and style that single-prompt generation rarely delivers, and AI evaluation misses obvious pixel-level failures.
Approach
End-to-end agentic pipeline: Claude agents for color interpretation, prompt generation, and Vision-based scoring; image generation tested across DALL-E 3 and gpt-image-1 with controlled theme/style probes to isolate the ceiling; deterministic pixel checks and a quality-gate retry as the final safety net.
Result
The unlock was a model switch, not more prompting. DALL-E 3 hit a ceiling on Bauhaus Minimal that spec iteration couldn't break; gpt-image-1 jumped past it on the same prompts. The pipeline then demonstrated clean transfer across new themes, palettes, and styles — generalizing beyond any single brief.
